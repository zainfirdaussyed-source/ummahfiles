Privacy policy

Last updated: 14 November 2026

What this app is

Video Uploader is a private tool that runs on its owner's own computer. The owner is its only user. It uploads the owner's videos to the owner's YouTube channels and reads the statistics of those videos.

What data it accesses

The app uses YouTube API Services. With the owner's consent it accesses, for the owner's own channels only:

  • permission to upload videos and set their titles, descriptions and thumbnails;
  • the list of the channel's videos;
  • view, like, comment, share and watch-time figures for those videos.

What it stores

The app stores a Google authorization token and the video statistics on the owner's computer. It has no server and no database of users. It collects no data from viewers or from anyone else.

Sharing

The app does not sell, share or transfer any data to third parties. Data goes only between the owner's computer and Google.

Removing access and data

Access can be revoked at any time on the Google security settings page: https://myaccount.google.com/permissions. Deleting the app's folder on the owner's computer deletes all stored data.

YouTube and Google terms

By using this app the user agrees to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.
